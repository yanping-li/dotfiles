# Words

## palette

**Pronunciation**: PAL-it (rhymes with "mallet")

**Meaning**: A set of colors, tools, or options available for use. Can refer to a physical board used by painters to mix colors, or figuratively to any range of choices or resources.

**Example usage**: "The designer chose a warm color palette for the website, using oranges and reds to create an inviting feel." or "The team had a palette of technologies to choose from for the project."

## typography

**Pronunciation**: ty-POG-rah-fee

**Meaning**: The art and technique of arranging type (letters, numbers, symbols) to make text readable and visually appealing. It involves choosing fonts, sizes, spacing, and layout to communicate effectively.

**Example usage**: "Good typography is essential for user interface design, making content easy to read and aesthetically pleasing." or "The website's typography featured a bold sans-serif header with a lighter serif body font."

## frontmatter

**Pronunciation**: FRONT-mat-er

**Meaning**: Metadata placed at the beginning of a document or file, typically in YAML or TOML format, enclosed by delimiters (usually `---`). It provides structured information about the document without being displayed as content.

**Example usage**: "The frontmatter in the markdown file contains the title, date, and author fields." or "I added frontmatter to my blog post to specify the publication date and categories."

## farm out

**Pronunciation**: farm OUT

**Meaning**: To delegate or outsource work to another person or organization; to distribute tasks or responsibilities to others rather than handling them yourself.

**Example usage**: "The manager decided to farm out the data analysis to a specialized consulting firm." or "We farmed out the graphic design work to freelancers so the in-house team could focus on core features."
